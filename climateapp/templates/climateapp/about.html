About Climate Monitor

Climate Monitor is a Django-based web application used to store and display climate information.

The application records temperature, humidity, rainfall and air quality information.

Objectives

  • Collect climate information.
  • Store environmental data.
  • Display climate information.
  • Compare climate conditions.
  • Allow users to add new climate records.
